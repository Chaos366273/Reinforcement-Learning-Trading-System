01 · Teach

Pick a universe and a style

Cautious, Balanced or Aggressive. Each maps to the real reward and risk settings in the training config. A mentor can warm-start the policy.

02 · Test

Exam on data it never trained on

The report card leads with deterministic results and shows the gap between training and a held-out window, so a lucky backtest cannot sneak through.

03 · Trade

Paper first. Live stays locked.

The bot paper-trades with stops, size caps and a written go-live checklist. Real-money routing is not available in this build.

Validation discipline

Train curve and exam curve stay on screen together. Overfit is a status, not a surprise.

Risk guardrails

Default max position 15% of the book, trailing stop, and a 25% drawdown halt. You set them before the first step.

Explainable decisions

Every paper trade opens into plain English plus the feature contributions that moved the policy.

Questions

Is this live trading?

No. Training and the desk use simulated paper money. Live routing stays locked behind the Oversight checklist and is marked coming later.

How fast does a bot learn on screen?

A full research run updates every rollout, not every tick. Quick Train is the live demo: a small universe aimed at a few minutes, replayed here from a recorded run.

Do I need to know reinforcement learning?

Simple mode speaks in sentences. Pro mode shows learning rate, entropy, clip range and SHAP values.
